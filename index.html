About us

What is the SCC?

The Sports Commentary Club (SCC) is an academic and media-focused student organization at Amador Valley High School. It builds real skills in public speaking, real-time critical analysis, and sports journalism. It is run by students, with President Arjun Prabhu and Vice President Will Bridenbaugh, and advised by George Kramer and Bryan Alamillo. The club is modeled on professional sports broadcasting networks and college communications programs.

The monthly rotation

How a month of meetings runs

Wk 1
Live Panel Roundtables
Timed, multi-person analyst panels debating hot topics across pro sports, like trade deadlines, franchise decisions and player brand strategy.
Wk 2
Play-by-Play & Broadcast Simulations
Workshops where you practice live game commentary, presentation pacing and post-game breakdowns using short game clips.
Wk 3
Business & Valuation Case Studies
The corporate side of sports: shoe deals (Nike vs. Adidas NIL strategies), stadium financing and franchise operations.
Wk 4
Guest Lectures & Peer Feedback
Bay Area media professionals, sports marketers and agents, followed by peer reviews of member broadcasts.

Our objectives

  • Run a weekly platform for analyzing pro sports management decisions, roster strategy and broadcast mechanics.
  • Stay fully compliant with AVHS ASB guidelines, with quarterly minutes and advisor oversight.
  • Field student teams at West Coast university sports-business summits and pitch invitationals.
Guidelines

Member expectations

Active participation

Engage in the discussions and play the games.

Respectful debates

Strong sports opinions, delivered respectfully. Respect everyone's ideas and never put people down.

Listening skills

Simply put: don't talk when others are speaking. Use active listening.

Not OK: name-calling during disagreements, being disruptive or rude, or leaving the classroom a mess. Repeated offenses have consequences. If you can't make a meeting, email the club ahead of time.
